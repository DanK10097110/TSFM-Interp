# Does matching eight outputs require learned recipient features?

The positive control results are compatible with a simple alternative: eight forecast values leave many editable hidden dimensions, and a controller with the recipient's local derivative can find a solution even in an untrained model. Test this directly, without fitting a receiver SAE. Keep the trained TimesFM donor, its 16 archived features, firm 20% receiver-radius limit, donor-derived output weight and the original 192 daily and 128 VM histories. Compare trained Chronos-Bolt-small with two architecture-matched receivers freshly initialized from the same config using the repository's `random_init` path and two predetermined seeds 7513 and 9749. Establish a separate median training-state norm in each receiver on the same 1,024 histories. Use each receiver's own ordinary response and subtract its own ordinary forecast; interventions are evaluated without using the quality of that forecast.

Match first-eight local derivatives using the original damped algorithm; apply the write and measure all 16 forecasts. Compare wrong lead order, full and horizon-centered recovery on leads 1–8, and the unobserved 9–16. Report cap count, unconstrained norm and Jacobian sensitivity in each receiver's own physical units. Identical budgets as a fraction of hidden-state norm need not yield equal output leverage. Validate finite differences and ordinary forecasts. A positive random-receiver result would falsify the interpretation that high in-horizon fidelity alone establishes shared *learned* features. A negative result at this budget leaves architecture, conditioning and learned weights entangled. This tests controllability; it makes no claim about forecasting quality of untrained checkpoints.

**Numerical amendment before scoring either random receiver.** The original symmetric 0.2%-radius check failed on the first random receiver (19.4% relative discrepancy). A diagnostic sweep over steps from 0.01% to 16% showed disagreement shrinking rapidly as the probe rose above float32 subtraction noise, then rising again at the largest step. Preserve the failed measurement and require all three neighboring 1%, 2%, and 4% steps to pass the original 8% error tolerance, identically for both random seeds and pretrained weights. The intervention, dose, held-out cases and scoring rule are unchanged.

**Output-scale follow-up.** After measuring much smaller output sensitivity in the random networks, ask whether their direction or their absolute amplitude is limiting. Use donor and receiver Jacobians on the 96 disjoint calibration histories. Pick one scalar per receiver so that the 80th percentile of raw requested write length on firing calibration cases equals 10% of its training radius, with a maximum multiplier of one. Scale the requested donor signature by this one constant on every held-out item and feature. Keep the original 20% write cap, solver, natural donor activity and endpoints. The reference is the scaled version of the original actual donor ablation; it represents a change of response amplitude, not a second measured removal of a smaller donor feature. Report physical RMS and the noise from an independently executed, same-batch zero write. Compare true vs wrong lead order and performance in the first eight vs next eight unconstrained leads. These scaled tasks cannot be used as evidence that a random model can reproduce the natural dose.
