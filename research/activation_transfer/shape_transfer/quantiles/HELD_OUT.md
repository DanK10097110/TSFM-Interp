# What happens between the three fitted quantiles?

Frozen before inspecting the distributional transfer scores. Apply the same native donor removal and the same median-only, three-quantile local, and three-quantile fixed recipient writes. Read out all nine publicly emitted deciles for leads 1–16. The solver has only the ordinary-state sensitivities of q10, q50 and q90 on leads 1–8. Withhold q20, q30, q40, q60, q70 and q80 across all leads. All quantities below are changes due to intervention. No realized future observations enter the analysis.

1. At each withheld decile remove that lead's median change. Test recovery of this remaining change in the interior of the forecast distribution. Report a privileged forecast-only reference obtained by linearly interpolating the *observed donor's* q10, q50 and q90 changes, and an accessible forecast-only reference interpolating the pre-intervention donor derivative estimate at those three deciles.
2. Subtract the piecewise linear interpolation of each model's own three anchor changes. The remaining two-side curvature measures detail invisible to all three anchor values. Recovery of that detail asks more than recovery of the shared shift and spread. A straight line between emitted quantile levels has precisely zero residual and zero recovery by construction. Report residual energy beside performance. Use equal source-atom weights and paired series bootstrap, retaining the existing ≥8-firings rule.
3. For leads 9–16, the test also withholds the time horizon. Report these separately. As a diagnostic, fit pooled, within-donor linear interpolation weights on 96 disjoint calibration histories from the same 16 prespecified real donor removals. Predict each *within-side* interior change relative to median from the signed change of the relevant tail relative to median, one slope per decile and lead. Include a single shared weight per decile pooled across all 16 leads as a less flexible control. Apply the learned weights to donor derivatives only at leads 1–8. For a privileged ceiling on the interpolator, also apply the weights to actual donor anchors. The receiver gets none of these additional gradients or measured effects. These controls distinguish what a matched forecast can accomplish from evidence that hidden distributional structure is shared.
