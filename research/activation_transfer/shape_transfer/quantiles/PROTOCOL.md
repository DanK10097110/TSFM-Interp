# Does a transferred median intervention also copy the donor's forecast uncertainty?

Registered before scoring quantile responses. Both pretrained checkpoints expose native 0.1, 0.5 and 0.9 quantiles. Previous cross-checkpoint controllers observed only median derivatives. An actual atom removal could raise the median, widen the interval, or change how the two tails move relative to the median. A constant translation of all quantiles predicts exactly zero change in width or asymmetry, even if median recovery is perfect.

Hold the donor feature IDs, natural amplitudes, post-block sites, final 32-observation token groups, 20% recipient cap, inputs, source SAE reconstruction residual and two checkpoints fixed. Use 96 calibration histories only for weighting and fixed operators. Evaluate separately on the original daily/VM and additional daily/traffic cohorts and on the original eight leads. Run the actual frozen models for every signed intervention. Do not use future observations. Study the checkpoints' emitted quantiles; the experiment does not validate the calibration of their predictive intervals.

Decompose three quantile changes into (1) median, (2) half-width = (q90 − q10)/2, and (3) asymmetry = (q90 + q10)/2 − q50. Test whether the earlier median-only adaptive solution copies the *unseen* two latter channels. Compare a per-history joint inverse allowed to see the three donor and receiver derivatives and a fixed joint recipient inverse fitted only on calibration, both applied to the donor's current signature. A negative control swaps which tail of the target is treated as upper versus lower in the solver, preserving median and asymmetry but reversing half-width. We also report a median-only/level-of-each-quantile baseline predicting zero half-width and asymmetry changes by construction.

Within each channel weight mean and centered lead changes for equal expected calibration energy under isotropic donor displacements. Then weight the three channels to equal total calibration energy. Use the established relative ridge .001 and physical cap for all methods. The median-only controller must replay its original median forecast changes. Report each channel's dose-sensitive skill across leads, full and centered, estimated per frequently firing source atom (at least eight examples/domain; at least 1e−5 RMS reference change). Show counts, RMS, output ordering/crossing rates, and paired series-bootstrap intervals. No supplied outcome from a perturbed test donor enters any solver. A strong median and weak unmeasured width would distinguish endpoint control from shared uncertainty processing; a successful joint inverse would show the receiver has control authority for that specified effect.
