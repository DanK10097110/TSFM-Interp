# Can a generic probabilistic forecast shape explain transfer to unseen deciles?

After finding appreciable recovery at six undisclosed deciles, the observed within-donor interpolation weights suggested a simple comparison. In a Gaussian location-scale family the quantile displacement relative to the median scales with the standard-normal quantile. Permit each side its own observed tail displacement. This prescribes the same scale-free six weights for all histories, features, checkpoints and leads, with zero fitting. A piecewise Gaussian approximation need not describe the models' ordinary predictive distributions; the experiment concerns *changes* under intervention.

Freeze this before running the comparison. Use the 128 M4 daily and 128 traffic histories from blind_frequency, never evaluated on these distributional outcomes. Keep the original donor features, sites, natural doses and local eight-lead three-quantile controller. Evaluate six unseen deciles on leads 1–8; 9–16 are a secondary temporal holdout. Report interior change relative to the median, and the more demanding residual after subtracting each write's own Gaussian interpolation at q10/q50/q90. Directly compare a no-intervention interpolation using the identical ordinary-state donor derivatives supplied to the controller, and another using the donor's true anchor changes as a privileged reference. Use per-feature skill and paired series and crossed feature–series intervals. The six weights derived by ordinary interpolation on the 96 other daily histories are frozen too.

Also test controllability of fine distributional shape. In a second physical arm expose ordinary-state q30 and q70 derivatives at leads 1–8. Add their departures from the Gaussian approximation as two separately calibrated channels in the local inverse, beside the original median, half-width and asymmetry. Balance the five channels using 96 separate calibration histories. Score the two newly disclosed deciles separately from the four still hidden; all rows and weighting rules are fixed in advance. A five-anchor advantage on the two fitted residuals would establish that fine detail can be controlled at these sites. A benefit on the other four residuals would require additional generalization. A loss in the median or tails is a possible cost of paying for these constraints. Report the training-radius cap counts. The daily and traffic item IDs are linked in the blind-frequency manifest, with previous study IDs excluded.

**Numerical alignment.** The independent median reference on the 256 new histories used one CPU thread. A two-thread Bolt replay differed on 131 of 1,048,576 entries at the original coordinatewise tolerance; global relative error was 0.00007868 and the worst per-history relative error was 0.001785. Repeating the identical calculation at one thread reproduced every entry exactly. Match the original two-thread settings on calibration and one-thread settings on confirmation. Keep the original tolerance and signed finite-difference criteria.

**Five-anchor interpolation comparison.** After seeing that the two additional constraints improved the four withheld deciles, compare the physical receiver with a forecast-only estimate given the same five ordinary-state donor derivatives. For q20/q40/q60/q80 interpolate between their two nearest exposed neighbors, in both probability and standard-normal quantile coordinates. Also give the interpolator measured donor anchors as a privileged comparison. Subtract each model's *own* five-point Gaussian interpolation from its four measured internal changes and score the residual separately. These retrospective contrasts on the two frozen cohorts ask whether the additional apparent generalization exceeds what interpolation from five anchors can already explain.
