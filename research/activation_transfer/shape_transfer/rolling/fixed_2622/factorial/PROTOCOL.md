# Daily phase and a one-week change of forecast origin on the same traffic sensors

Frozen before selecting new windows or viewing their responses. The earlier traffic comparison moved histories 168–335 hours earlier by a different amount on each sensor. It changed both time-of-day phase and calendar placement. The present comparison holds the 96 previously declared verification sensors and their ordering fixed and uses only TimesFM donor 2362 and the Bolt receiver. This is a follow-up on the same sensors. Only the aligned tail cell repeats the original tail windows.

For each sensor construct 480 observed hourly values at an anchor and a current origin 32 observations later. The four cells end 0, 12, 168 and 180 observations before the original tail schedule. Standardize both windows in each cell by that cell's anchor mean and SD. Record raw exclusive start/stop indices and sensor IDs. An offset of 168 hours preserves the relative hour and day of the week; a 12-hour offset rotates the daily cycle but also shifts the calendar time by half a day. These fixed windows can test sensitivity to the two scheduled time offsets. They cannot distinguish a daily cycle from every other change a half-day earlier, or estimate across-week generality from two weeks of one traffic data panel.

Three physical interventions at the new origin, using the same target radius and historical positive-activation fallback as the existing 2622 study. (1) Write Bolt decoder 2622 with a positive sign fixed a priori and coefficient equal to its anchor allowed bound, regardless of current support. (2) Choose one of the 32 currently firing Bolt atoms, fit the signed coefficient against the source's local linearized natural removal and cap at that candidate's own natural current budget. (3) Choose the existing first calibration-matched sham ID 1355, give it exactly the physical norm of the positive 2622 write, and take its sign from the anchor source and receiver derivatives. No source or target intervention outcome enters any policy. Compare with the current *measured nonlinear* eight-lead TimesFM 2362 removal. Include positive amounts when 2622 is dormant, exact raw donor amplitudes and current/old receiver codes.

Primary sample is intersection of sensors where 2362 fires at anchor and current in all four cells, so comparisons refer to the same sensors. Report all-cell and per-cell firing coverage. Compute full and within-eight centered recovery, with paired whole-sensor resampling of all four cells (4,000 replicates). Primary outcome is fixed minus fresh centered recovery. Report this advantage separately per cell, the two conditional half-day and two conditional week effects, the two balanced main effects and the difference in differences, as well as equal-norm sham comparison. Keep the estimator, scope and uncertainty even if effects reverse. Source is a single atom, sensor intervals do not cover donor or calendar-time uncertainty. Compare the 0-offset physical output to the prior 96-sensor archive after accounting for the explicitly positive fixed policy.

Before any new factorial policy outcomes were available, add a descriptive scale check to the same three physical arms. Report actual source and receiver response RMS, pooled signed cosine and receiver/source norm ratio for each cell, with the exact identity recovery = 2 × cosine × norm ratio − norm ratio². This separates agreement in direction from response size in the observed scores. Also express the fixed-versus-fresh squared-error difference against the same average source energy across the four schedules as a denominator sensitivity.
