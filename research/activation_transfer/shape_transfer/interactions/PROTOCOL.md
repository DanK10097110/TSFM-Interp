# Do two independently transferred SAE removals interact like the originals?

An eight-lead controller can match each of two source writes in isolation. If it also preserves how the writes *interact*, that would give stronger evidence of a common local computation: the controller never sees the effect of their joint removal. Freeze before evaluating any joint outcome. Use every coactive pair among the original 16 source atoms in each of the two donor dictionaries. Choose no pairs by their effects. Compare cross-model translation and translation back into the same site of the donor through the same eight-output interface. The original 192 daily, 128 additional daily, 128 VMs and 128 traffic histories retain their roles; summarize each separately, including insufficiently observed cells.

For each source pair A and B measure clean, A alone, B alone and A+B together through the actual model on the same batch. The interaction is the joint change minus the sum of the two individually measured changes. It is the change in the causal effect of B when A has already been removed. Compute the same four forecasts after transporting each direction independently using the usual source and recipient first-eight Jacobians at the *ordinary* input. Apply one common nonnegative factor across both single target displacements and their sum whenever their maximum norm exceeds 20% of the receiver's training radius. This makes the target single and joint arms a consistent factorial intervention. A purely linear receiving model has exactly zero interaction even when the shared cap is binding. Source removals use the original full coefficients in all arms. Report unclipped subsets and frequency of clipping. Self and cross arms may require different target caps; compare the intervals conditional on the same source pairs.

Primary endpoints are (i) recovery of the full joint early median waveform, (ii) recovery of the *interaction alone* at those same leads, both also scored after removing the per-case horizon mean, and (iii) the size of the source interaction relative to the individual contributions. A forecast-only oracle adds the two measured source singleton effects. Its error on the joint estimates how much linear additivity alone explains. Compare receiver interaction with the zero-interaction baseline and with a global sign reversal diagnostic, and repeat beyond lead eight. Use equal weights per source pair that cofires on at least eight distinct histories in a domain. Estimate uncertainty by resampling whole histories; pairs that share an atom must not be treated as independent replicates. Compare interaction RMS to same-batch zero-write noise acquired independently. Check that every source singleton replays the previous physical reference. The experiment concerns subtraction of two SAE decoder contributions from an intact residual state at their ordinary coefficients. It does not recompute the SAE after removing the first feature.

**Matched factorial reference.** Prior to scoring joint outcomes, the independent reviewer identified a numerical-baseline distinction. Every nonzero arm patches the cached token state. Subtract the zero-displacement forecast run through precisely that same patch path for all three arms. For differences recorded relative to an ordinary unpatched forward, this adds the saved zero-patch offset once to the interaction. The true ordinary and same-batch zero measurements remain available for sensitivity checks.
