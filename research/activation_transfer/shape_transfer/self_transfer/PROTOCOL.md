# Is the missing continuation a cross-model problem?

A recipient controlled on eight leads often disagrees with the donor on the next eight. Two explanations are observationally confounded in a cross-model comparison. Its architecture might read the future differently, or the eight disclosed numbers might already have discarded the source information needed to identify the original write. Test the second explanation by transporting through the *same* eight-lead interface back into the donor at precisely its original site. The self recipient receives the same current Jacobian and an eight-component source signature, with no access to the donor decoder vector when constructing its write. A direct native removal is the observable perfect-reference intervention.

Use three trained checkpoints as both donors and recipients: Chronos-Bolt-small, TimesFM 2.5 and Timer-base-84m. Complete the 3×3 panel. For Timer as a new donor use its published middle-block SAE, the final 96-step token, and the earlier sixteen-atom selection algorithm on exactly the same 1,024 training histories and the seed used for the archive donors. No held-out responses or feature identities enter selection. For Bolt and TimesFM reuse the trained SAEs, last 32-step data window and frozen feature draws. Timer emits a deterministic point, the other two emit their medians. Compare actual 16-lead forecasts on 192 original daily, 128 additional daily, 128 virtual-machine, and 128 traffic histories. Use the previous relative damping, shape weight estimated per donor on 96 separate histories, and 20% of training-state radius as a cap. Report cap counts and the cosine between the self-recovered direction and actual decoder row.

Primary endpoints are the self recipient's centered recovery of the full-dose native source removal, separately on leads 1–8 and 9–16. The prespecified control for external comparisons is the *same source and same histories*, comparing a self recipient with each other recipient. The earlier two-model off-diagonal cells should replay the preceding results. Summarize paired history intervals conditional on the selected features, keeping the eight-firing-history threshold, and report the newly selected Timer feature counts. A poor self continuation at strong early recovery would demonstrate a loss that does not require crossing architectures. A material remaining self advantage would identify an additional cross-model penalty for this editing rule. For a direct check against shrinkage artifacts, additionally execute exact, unregularized, uncapped self projection into the current row space. Compute the residual direction orthogonal to all eight early source derivatives and execute that component alone. Keep this residual write at its original magnitude; it may be much longer than the minimal write. If it moves later leads while it has exactly zero early linear effect, it is a constructive witness of hidden information discarded by the interface. Compare its real early effects to quantify nonlinear leakage.
