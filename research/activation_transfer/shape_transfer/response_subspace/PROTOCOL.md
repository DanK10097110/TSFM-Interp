# Can different hidden directions be functionally equivalent on new histories?

Registered before measuring the proxy interventions. Use the previously frozen 16-feature draws in each of three independent SAE dictionaries per trained checkpoint. Keep every donor feature identity, naturally firing history and coefficient fixed. The intervention reference is removing its original decoder contribution in the donor model. Construct alternative physical writes *in that same model*; this experiment asks what makes two hidden directions functionally interchangeable, a condition relevant to interpreting successful cross-model transfer. No paired second backbone is needed to fit the source subspace.

The eight rows of the mean calibration forecast Jacobian span directions whose effects are visible on average. Using 96 daily calibration histories, extend those eight directions with the leading variation in history-specific Jacobians orthogonal to that span. Weight mean versus centered eight-lead sensitivity as in the original donor calibration, and keep either 8, 32 or 96 axes. Compare removing the projected part of a source decoder to a foil that preserves its projection and scrambles the remainder by a fixed orthogonal change of basis in the complement. The foil preserves *every decoder norm and pairwise inner product in the sampled dictionary*, and its mean linear response on calibration is identical to the original feature to numerical precision. The 32-dimensional controls use clean-state PCA from the original 1,024 training histories and a seeded isotropic subspace. Neither uses forecast derivatives.

Actually run the original and altered writes at the original source activation for the 192+128 old daily/VM and 128+128 additional daily/traffic histories. Capture native 0.1, 0.5 and 0.9 forecast quantiles for 16 leads with every write. The primary endpoint is horizon-centered median recovery on leads 1–8. The source subspace never sees late leads or outer quantile derivatives; secondary endpoints are late centered median and early and late forecast half-width. Report retained hidden norm, norm-matched foil and clean-PCA checks, conditional and two-way series/feature uncertainty. Apply the data-independent rank-8 and rank-32 rules in all three dictionaries; the 96-axis and random controls are additional for the archive dictionary. The experiment concerns causal effects of the selected frozen directions and does not assert that rotations produce natural SAE features. Poor recovery of an exactly mean-matched foil would show that average forecast signatures miss history-specific distinctions. Strong recovery with a small response subspace, especially out of sample, would identify a reproducible sense in which different hidden directions can be equivalent.
