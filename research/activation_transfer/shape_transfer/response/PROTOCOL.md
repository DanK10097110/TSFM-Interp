# Can generic forecast-response supervision transport a source write?

Specified before running new probes or scoring new interventions. Reuse the original Bolt and TimesFM middle sites, 192-point standardized histories, eight-step forecast, 640 clean PCA-alignment histories, 64 daily response-probe FIT histories and 128+128 held-out daily/weekly histories. Collect each frozen model’s actual symmetric response around its clean state along 24 orthonormal PCs of clean alignment states, at the original physical radius .01 times the median clean state norm. Dividing the half difference by step defines J_m[i,h,k] in normalized forecast units per absolute hidden unit. Publish per-item arrays and IDs. The original 32 output-calibration histories are unused. No held-out forecast labels enter any map.

Stack 64×8 derivative rows for each model and solve a multioutput linear response map W = (J_tᵀJ_t+λI)⁻¹J_tᵀJ_s, with λ=.01 times the largest squared singular value of stacked J_t. This fixed 24×24 map receives only the source write’s known PC coefficients at use time. It receives no requested waveform or observed test source effect. It aims to reproduce a source intervention’s local forecast effect in the recipient. It uses target intervention responses on FIT and has more supervision than the clean-state ridge map. Use one fitted W per direction for every request.

Evaluate five requests of RMS .05. Three are the original lift, linear tilt and period-eight sine. Select two new shapes in advance: a localized early Gaussian hump centered at lead 2, width .85 leads, and a period-four sine. Sequentially project these two forms off the span of all earlier waveforms and renormalize. Their signed vectors are orthogonal to the original three and to each other. A source native policy for each waveform is the original ridge objective on its generic FIT Jacobian, limited to .20 times the source’s median clean norm. Use the original three native directions exactly; the two new directions consume no waveform-specific intervention observations. Independently fitted target native policies are labeled positive controls supplied the requested waveform. The two new waveform directions are held out of the map’s training and are new ask-shapes, not new fit histories.

Preserve a common recipient trust radius of .20 times its median clean state norm for all primary actual interventions; report every preclip and applied norm. Compare generic response transport with the clean-state ridge map at its capped natural size and at the generic response edit’s actual norm. Compare to two generic negative/positive controls. In the lead-order control permute the source’s eight lead rows by a single fixed nonidentity permutation [3,6,1,4,7,2,5,0] in every FIT history during map fitting. In the donor-free control replace the source J for each FIT history with the training mean source J, preserving population average behavior and the recipient’s item-specific fitting penalty. These two maps are evaluated at their own capped size and additionally norm-matched to the actual generic response edit. Score the actual recipient forecast after a one-sided write, relative to its ordinary forecast; exclude mathematical replay noise for an exactly zero dose.

The original desired request remains an external endpoint. Also compare actual recipient changes to the actual source-native changes on the same held-out histories. For each reference report 1−SSE/reference energy, signed projected gain and cosine, aggregated across histories and leads. For source mimicry include a horizon-centered diagnostic, across-item mean-subtracted scores and a donor-free forecast template fitted from the FIT source Jacobian, which is a forecast predictor and performs no target write. This prevents a match to a common waveform alone from being interpreted as history-specific transfer. Show recipient-native and source-native request feasibility, all five individual waves, actual dose and the clean comparator. Estimate paired 95% percentile intervals from 4,000 whole-series draws, conditional on fixed models/maps. Four primary comparisons test full source-mimic skill averaged equally over the two novel requests against the **dose-matched clean map** across two directions and two domains, with one-sided 1.25th percentile Bonferroni bounds. All other comparisons are descriptive. No held-out dose fitting, named-cue transport labels, or hyperparameter search.
