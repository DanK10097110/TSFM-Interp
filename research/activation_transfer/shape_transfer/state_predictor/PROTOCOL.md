# Predicting a donor write's effect without probing the current donor

Registered before collecting derivative memory or scoring predictions. Can a clean representation retrieve the local derivative of a real donor forecaster well enough to physically reproduce its SAE edit in a different frozen forecaster? Use Bolt ↔ TimesFM, original 480-point input, eight median leads, middle SAE post-block, final 32 observed inputs, and identical live direct source subtraction. A proposed source unit decoder direction is multiplied by the source feature's natural positive coefficient; every method knows feature identity/direction and that coefficient. Predictor-specific information below describes the lookup key beyond this common information.

**Information and splits.** Assemble 1,024 new generic full-hidden donor Jacobian observations, one per original M4 training history. Using the same histories that selected and trained the sparse dictionary is intentional; no feature-removal forecasts enter this memory. Use the independent original 96 calibration histories to select memory hyperparameters using generic Jacobian error. Train and calibration IDs are disjoint from evaluation. Freeze the same 192 daily, 128 machine, 128 extra daily and 128 extra traffic items as the depth study, and the 16 separately training-selected source features under both archived and retrained seed 1 dictionaries. Score each domain separately with at least eight observed positive source firings per feature. The eval cohorts have been examined in prior studies and the lookup outcomes are new.

**Four lookup keys, one estimator.** Use raw standardized 480 observations, pooled dense donor hidden state, all nonnegative SAE activation magnitudes (TopK), and its binary positive support. Use squared Euclidean distance on the unmodified vectors for each key, deterministic training-index tiebreaks. Binary distance is the number of differing active atoms. For each query, average the generic donor Jacobians of the nearest 1, 4, 16, 64 or 256 training items. Shrink each resulting predictor towards the mean training Jacobian with one scalar constrained to [0,1]. For each neighborhood calculate the analytic least-squares shrinkage on the 96 calibration histories; choose the neighborhood of lowest calibration error. Calibration loss is Frobenius error of the complete generic eight-row local derivative after the original donor mean/shape balancing; it sees no specified atoms or ablations. Include one independently shuffled association of training keys to derivative labels (seed 20261121081) for each key, with identical tuning against **correctly associated** calibration derivative labels. This is a conservative comparably tuned correspondence control. Training/calibration reference scales, means and splits stay unchanged. Refit the sparse-key hyperparameters for seed 1 because its dictionary changes; raw/dense fits and model Jacobian/receiver library are reusable.

**Response and physical intervention.** Multiply the retrieved donor Jacobian by the selected donor decoder direction. At evaluation only the oracle accesses the donor's true current Jacobian, and only for its own arm or scoring diagnostic. No current recipient derivatives are accessible. Use the original dense eight-vector recipient library fitted once on the 96 target calibration Jacobians; use its physical units, exact donor lead weights, original relative damping and per-case 0.2 median training-state radius cap. Compare raw, dense, sparse magnitudes, support, shuffled keys, constant mean and true-current donor with the same receiver. Apply actual nonlinear receiver patches to eligible and other firing cases and record the true donor SAE removal. When two methods yield identical requested writes reuse the identical measured response, recording this. Also score the donor's forecast-only prediction against its actual removal, and the predicted unit derivative against the true derivative for diagnosis; these forecast-space references are explicitly separate from physical transfer.

**Endpoints.** Primary: physical horizon-centered skill over eight leads against the donor's actual removal. Compute feature-wise normalized SSE on that feature's cases, then average eligible features equally; zero means predicting no effect. Also report full skill, mean and current-derivative controls, per-arm cap fractions, and paired differences vs mean, matched shuffle and dense reference. Bootstrap eval histories and eligible features independently with shared draws across methods; also retain fixed-feature series intervals. Primary comparisons are seed-0 dense vs mean and seed-0 sparse magnitudes vs dense across four domains × two directions (16 comparisons), with one-sided .003125 lower bounds and ordinary 95% intervals. A sparse advantage is only supported by the second contrast; report uncertainty even for nulls. Seed 1 checks direction and scale as an independently initialized dictionary, with separate selected atom identities. Give practical costs of the memory and clean-state read. Recomputing or tuning the recipient per test history is out of scope.
