# Can an entirely static late response support a physical write?

Chosen after scoring only the original daily/machine cohorts. No new-cohort forecast outcomes had been inspected. In both original daily directions the history-shuffled prior closely followed the paired prior, independently calibrated receiver synthesis scored higher, and the donor's static forecast template was almost as effective as its within-model history regression. A forecast-only template leaves open whether this simple signal can be written to a recipient.

Add one physical arm. Keep current first-eight donor and recipient derivatives as in the independent synthesis arm. Replace each model's predicted late derivative with **its own constant mean late Jacobian** on the original 96 calibration histories. Use the exact same per-atom source write, weighted 16-coordinate solve, cap and actual 16-lead forward. This arm requires no matching between source and recipient histories, and no predicted per-item variation in late derivatives. Compare dynamic independent synthesis minus static synthesis on centered late shape in the two new cohorts/directions, with four one-sided Bonferroni lower bounds at .0125. Report both their levels and differences; wide intervals cannot establish equivalence. Also report static synthesis versus paired to interpret how much behavior can be physically reproduced by an unpaired, static late model. Do not create additional arms.
