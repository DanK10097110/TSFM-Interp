# Why was the named pair silent on the traffic histories?

Follow-up registered after the primary data showed zero active cases on the 128 traffic sensors at clean or ±.25 relative-mean edits. Inspection of input covariates alone showed clean relative means strongly shifted (median 1.084 SD in daily and −.079 in traffic). Before viewing named ranks and actual conditional margins, freeze the following comparisons on the same 256 histories. They explain a cross-domain observation and do not constitute an independently confirmed new causal mechanism.

1. For clean and ±.25 isolated relative-mean inputs obtain each named feature’s signed preactivation, original SAE output, descending rank (one plus the count strictly higher), and signed margin to max(0, the Kth highest OTHER feature preactivation). Thus a positive margin predicts positive native TopK firing, subject to ties. Report the separate threshold and preactivation distributions and compare the measured actual code to the sign rule. Show natural recent-mean range and central 90% intersection and counts, in each domain, plus how many natural firings are represented on common support.
2. Match each traffic context to its nearest daily context (with replacement), based on observed native clean inputs only. Primary caliper is absolute recent-mean difference ≤.10 SD. Secondary calipers require differences ≤.20 recent mean, ≤.35 last value, and ≤.35 fitted rise. For the second match minimize squared distance normalized by those calipers. Report eligible traffic items, unique daily donors, code/rate/margin contrasts, worst/mean gaps, and 2,000 separate within-domain bootstraps that redo matching. Describe marginal and joint overlap rather than extrapolating a domain effect when few unique donors support a comparison.
3. Impose the exact same isolated relative mean on every feasible history in both domains: either 0 or +1.25 clean-history SD, holding native last value and slope. Then impose these same two means while also setting the endpoint relative to full mean to +.5 SD and fitted last-32 rise to zero in both domains. Preserve full-history mean and variance and the original normalized residual direction as in the original design. Require both targets feasible for every included case in a comparison; report counts and achieved coordinates. Positive and low targets are common-coordinate counterfactuals rather than symmetric shifts from every native history. The high target is in the central range of daily natural histories and is unusual for traffic. Across all four settings inspect firing, rank, margin and decomposition into preactivation and threshold, with whole-series uncertainty for high-minus-low and independent cohort difference for matched target settings. This discriminates sufficiency of the named coordinates for firing from hidden dependence on other preserved history structure. It cannot identify which remaining input statistic explains any residual difference. No altered model or forecast-grid tuning.
