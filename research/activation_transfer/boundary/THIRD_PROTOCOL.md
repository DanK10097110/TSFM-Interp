# Third-checkpoint replication

The two original dictionaries differ in support volatility and benefit from a fixed set of learned axes. Test the frozen smooth-gate rule in a third backbone, the public deterministic Amazon Chronos-2 forecaster. Use its middle `encoder.block.5` site and the archived public-development SAE already fitted there, selected before loading its response metrics. Its noncausal encoder includes forecast placeholders. Keep 64-step forward geometry as used during SAE training and retain the first eight steps of the median forecast for the downstream comparisons. Use exactly the same 32-step end-of-history alignment and the original paired M4 calibration/validation items. Prespecify both directions between Chronos-2 and each of Bolt and TimesFM. On weekly and Bitbrains, assess whether hard vs shoulder accuracy moves in the same direction as on the two original networks. The primary third-checkpoint contrast uses Chronos-2 as source with each of the two target dense changes. Keep all prior penalties, budgets and input-only controls. Report actual weekly same-site target forecast effects with native errors and signed cosine, ordinary input and the shuffle. This extends a study with previously examined series to an unseen checkpoint, and both Chronos models share an organizational lineage. No model is selected by its results.
