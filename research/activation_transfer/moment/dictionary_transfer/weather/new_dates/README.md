# A receiving dictionary can build a good edit, but deciding when to apply it is harder

Four MOMENT-large features were chosen for their firing frequency in the historical M4 training data. We reused the directions and receiver-only dose predictor from the original weather test without any fitting or threshold changes. This test uses another 35 USCRN stations and eleven new forecast origins, all after release. The 385 histories yielded 1,540 feature opportunities, including 568 actual source firings. The original donor identities, model, SAE seeds, group coefficients, forecast head and intervention site were fixed. |---|---:|---:|
| One matched atom with its own dose | −1.796 [−3.200, −.005] | −1.532 |
| Fixed 16-atom group, dose inferred from recipient codes | .639 [−.837, .715] | .799 [.578, .845] |
| Same group, actual dose supplied by donor | .859 [.688, .897] | .859 [.688, .897] |

The donor-dose group outperformed the single on each of the eleven dates. Its paired all-opportunity advantage was 2.655 [.788, 4.063]. The receiver-only improvement over the single was 2.434 [−.095, 3.747], positive on seven dates. The donor-dose group also beat receiver-only dosing on all eleven dates. The direction is a reliable substitute for these four selected features when given the right strength. Evidence that the second dictionary can decide independently when to use it is sensitive to date and to how false alarms are counted. [All scores, per-date, per-atom, subset and unit sensitivities](results/summary.json)

The inferred policy detected 539 of 568 donor firings, alongside 424 false positives. The natural matched atom detected 259 with 42 false positives. For feature 1867 the receiver-only policy caught 92 of 93 but applied a further 202 mistaken edits, and the naturally matched atom's own-dose recovery was −6.947. Leaving that feature out changes the natural single's macro score from −1.796 to −.079. The four receiver-only point estimates were .633, .862, .513 and .546, although whole-date resampling still includes weak outcomes. On the predeclared all-opportunity endpoint the data support the donor-dose result most clearly. Restricting to donor firings discards the forecast cost of applying an unnecessary edit.

All runs use actual downstream native forecasts. Forty-eight independently selected full native replay checks agreed with the prefix-cached results to within .000006°C. Repeating the macro comparison with centered changes in raw °C gives −1.970, .629 and .857. The primary metric divides each series by its own historical SD before computing each donor's squared-error recovery. All four donors receive equal weight.
