# Why can a group of 16 SAE directions reproduce a donor removal?

**For four historically frequent features in MOMENT-large, matching the group's angle and length relative to the true donor was sufficient to reproduce its level of forecast agreement.** This test follows the observation that a sparse group beat a norm-matched rotated dictionary. Rotation had also made it harder to point toward the donor, so the original control could not tell whether the learned group had an additional advantage once geometric accuracy was equal. [Geometric control fixed before these writes](PROTOCOL.md) · [Original group and rotated-dictionary comparison](../README.md)

On the 35-station, eleven-date panel we decomposed each real group vector into its component parallel to the donor decoder and its perpendicular residual. We retained the parallel component and the exact residual length and randomly oriented that residual perpendicular to the donor. Four draws per feature were held fixed across the entire panel. A separate control borrowed another nominated feature's real group residual and projected it into the required perpendicular space. All controls match the real group's length, cosine to the donor and Euclidean error. They use the same actual donor dose. The donor/group cosines ranged from .861 to .899. The procedure gives the investigator the donor direction explicitly. The random controls are dense hidden writes, whereas the learned group can be expressed through 16 directions of the independently trained dictionary.

| Centered forecast-change recovery | Real 16-term group | Mean score of four angle-matched random writes | Real minus random, crossed station/date 95% |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Full H16 | .859 | .893 | −.034 [−.175, −.002] |
| Days 1–8 | .835 | .885 | −.050 [−.391, .006] |
| Days 9–16 | .897 | .879 | .018 [−.044, .048] |

H16 scores for the four random draws were .849, .907, .923 and .893. The borrowed real residual scored .802. For three of four features the learned group beat the mean random residual, by .027, .002 and .052. For feature 1867 it fell behind by .217, producing the overall negative contrast. Uncertainty resamples station and date with donors and control directions fixed. This comparison gives no estimate of the distribution of all possible group directions or source features. [Per-feature, time-resolved and physical-size results](results/summary.json)

The learned dictionary provided a **sparse way to get close to the donor**. The resulting waveform is compatible with other vectors at the same angle. Reproducing it alone gives little evidence that the exact 16 selected features are a unique mechanism, or that they would fire together naturally. On all 1,540 opportunities the source removal's uncentered RMS was .084°C, the real group's .092°C, and the four random controls' .066–.079°C. Identical geometry also permits differences in raw forecast magnitude and in individual leads.
