# Can two independent sparse dictionaries transfer an intervention?

Freeze before comparing any dictionary match. Two MOMENT checkpoints, two separately initialized TopK SAEs per checkpoint, 1,024 disjoint fitting histories with 60 observed patches each and an existing evaluation panel of 128 M4 daily and 128 traffic histories. All forecasts use H16, the middle block and the last observed patch, never a future placeholder. The 16 source atoms per SAE were selected by a fixed training-only procedure. Evaluate each source on the same events where its coefficient is positive. Require at least eight histories per reported atom per domain. Two dictionary seeds are a sensitivity check and share data.

**Fixed matching.** The primary dictionary matcher chooses the target atom of maximum training Pearson correlation between positive-clipped sparse codes on 1,024 paired last-observed patches. An independent check uses decoder orientation: fit a ridge map from paired source to target hidden states on the 60 observed positions of a fixed subsample of training histories. Fit centered linear slopes on training only, choose ridge on disjoint training-reserved items and keep the bias out of displacement transfer. Under the same frozen encoder the geometry map is the identity. For an input source removal, compare the matched target atom at (a) the target's actual gate and dose, (b) a forced write using the transported vector's norm, and the transported dense vector itself. Also test the single target atom closest in decoder cosine to the transported vector, using the same norm. A deterministic random eligible target atom with the same norm is a negative control. Record collisions, firing overlap, physical norm, target/donor shape, and uncentered scale. Reusing a source decoder direction within its original frozen network is the positive control.

**Behavioral comparison.** Fit the first eight source derivatives through the recipient's current derivative, with radius 20% of its typical training activation norm. These two local operators use the ordinary, unperturbed receiving and source histories. No optimized write sees the donor's actual intervention or its last eight values. Archive the receiving final states under each write so empirical comparisons can be repeated under the independent interior readout. The primary comparisons use the released native heads, and the response matched solve uses native outputs. Report centered and full first/second-block skill against the source's actual full finite removal. At identical encoder, compare the two seeds; across encoder sizes, compare both directions. Skills of 1/0 mean exact/unchanged output. An unstable match with physical effect near the precision floor is not evidence of a distinct semantic feature. Separately report target support Jaccard for codes and whether the target atom would naturally have fired. This experiment tests atomic match / dense mapping / task-conditioned control, not semantic universality.

Comparisons will be summarized by source atom and history, retaining all arms, sources and domains. Same-encoder comparisons deliberately hold weights and ordinary forecasts identical to isolate dictionary choice. Between checkpoints, the native small readout has known forecast quality limitations.
