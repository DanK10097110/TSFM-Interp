# Does the official MOMENT forecasting-head architecture change the core-model choice?

A locally fitted MOMENT-large forecasting head improved substantially over the released mask-and-reconstruct forecast on M4 daily. Persistence still gave the lowest error among the methods in the table on that domain. Across six data sources the general-purpose forecasters and the history-selected classical baseline had substantially lower errors than the probe. This supports retaining the published large masked pathway for the existing, explicitly scoped interpretability studies. The authors’ separate frequency-specific training experiments address a different question. [Frozen protocol](PROTOCOL.md) · [MOMENT paper, Table 3 and Appendix F.1.2](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.03885v3)

## The matched experiment

The same 1,024 historical M4 daily series trained each local head, and a separate 128 selected ridge strength. Of the 480 values in each record, the first 464 entered the model and the final 16 were targets only. The input means, scales and all 18,432 normalized training/validation labels matched the earlier masked-terminal experiment exactly. The six-domain evaluation used all 480 observed values at both origins for each of 96 held-out items per domain. The post-release weather evaluation used all four 2026 origins for each of 46 NOAA stations. Historical benchmark datasets may overlap encoder pretraining. [Training and validation](results/fit.json) · [Earlier masked-head protocol](../head_test/PROTOCOL.md) · [Weather panel](../forecast_quality/post_release/PROTOCOL.md)

We used the public forecasting task’s complete flattened output from the frozen large encoder, 64 positions × 1,024 channels. All 16 output leads have separate rows in a 1,048,592-parameter linear head including its intercept. The official direct architecture predicts the normalized future and reverses visible-history normalization. The separately registered residual arm fits deviations from the last observation and adds that observation back through a raw-input skip. In lag ridge, this changes the shrinkage target. With hidden states it additionally supplies an explicit observed quantity outside the hidden readout. Both sets of readout weights are local. The masked-terminal comparator shares one 8-coordinate decoder across two placeholders, giving two patch examples per training series. The flattened head has one 16-output example per series. Both consume exactly the same 16 scalar labels. ## What pathway would a new interpretability experiment measure?

The public forecasting dispatch and the original short-horizon linear-probing research dispatch produced **identical fitted raw forecasts** on independently replayed cases. All 222 non-head state tensors matched the pinned safetensors exactly. Physically installing the fitted weights and calling the public model on 96 held-out benchmark origins and 32 weather origins reproduced both cached arms within **2.20×10⁻⁶ history SD**. Replacing two training series’ excluded labels with values increased by ten million changed zero encoder coordinates and zero forecasts. Independent explicit item/origin loops reproduced the reported held-out errors within 9×10⁻¹⁶. [Native audit](results/audit.json) · [Independent score computation](score.py)

The released reconstruction route computes the H16 readout from two future placeholder patches with a shared decoder. The forecasting route reads every encoder output and has no future placeholders. The input/attention masks exclude padding from RevIN and from the attention keys. The flattened head still receives the final padding queries as inputs. On two replayed histories, changing only masked padding from edge values to zero changed **zero observed-state coordinates** but changed padding-state coordinates by as much as 0.499. Their decoder columns alone reproduced the forecast change within 7.5×10⁻⁷ SD. A unit intervention in the first lead’s gradient direction changed lead one by 0.0260 SD at the first padding slot and 0.3038 SD at the final observed slot. The direct head placed 1.75% of its squared weights on the four evaluation padding slots and 29.95% on the final four observed slots. These shares measure decoder geometry; correlated hidden states also matter to prediction. [Physical state and padding checks](results/audit.json)

On the preregistered stratified subset, removing the earliest 16 observations to restore training length changed the average forecast by 0.037–0.055 history SD across datasets. Holding the whole 480-value input fixed and changing masked padding to zero moved it 0.027–0.073 SD. Daily subset MASE was 3.331 at ordinary length 480, 3.347 when cropped to 464, and 3.524 with zero padding. These limited checks show a real padding pathway and little evidence that the two-patch length mismatch explains the primary accuracy gap. A forecast-head SAE analysis would need its own ordinary input mask, token alignment, fitted readout, calibration population and held-out validation. Existing masked-token causal effects belong to the reconstruction computation. [Stratified sensitivity results](results/audit.json) · [Commands, verification and published sources](REPRODUCE.md)
