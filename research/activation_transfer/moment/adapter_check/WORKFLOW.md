# Running MOMENT in the lens pipeline

For experiments involving a native public forecast head, use MOMENT-1-large with its explicit revision in the supplied large config. Small remains useful for testing the published small checkpoint and as a contrasting encoder/readout. All three publicly released weight files and the momentfm package carry an MIT license designation. [Large workflow config](../../../../tsfm_lens/configs/moment_large.yaml) · [Small config](../../../../tsfm_lens/configs/moment.yaml) · [Release and package provenance](README.md)

From `tsfm_lens/`, with the benchmark loader and the optional moment dependency present, the ordinary production commands are

```bash
python run.py --config configs/moment_large.yaml --stages corpus,extract,l0,sae,steering
python run_sae_ablation.py --run runs/moment_large --all --top-k-series 4 --n-null-directions 6 --max-series 8 --keep-forecasts 4 --n-features-per-rule 2
```

The SAE is fitted to post-block observation states. Use eight-step analysis windows to give its training rows the same granularity as a native token ablation. A variance-selected and a random candidate rule are enabled. The profile-matched null removes a random decoder direction with the size of that candidate’s own edit at every token. Six nulls and four histories per feature exercise the implementation and have little precision for scientific attribution. Preserve the standard dictionary admission gate when interpreting feature effects.

The separate steering stage requests a level shift of 0.05 history SD and replays the internal edit. The generic tracer recognizes parameter-owning modules with 2-D or 3-D inputs. The public MOMENT reconstruction linear layer receives a four-dimensional tensor `[B,1,64,d]`. The configured site instead uses the three-dimensional input to `encoder.final_layer_norm` and writes a shared direction across positions. The learned request solver and SAE-feature ablation target different scientific quantities. Since the native head is point-valued, the config uses a single median channel. There is no native interval-width or asymmetry effect to interpret.

For the normal-command integration check we first used 64 sealed public-dev histories, 32-step pooled windows, and a 128-atom K=8 SAE at the middle blocks. The held-out fidelities were 0.707 small and 0.419 large and both failed admission. In particular, the large eight-series held-out mean token-replacement ΔMASE was +0.756. A preliminary 256-atom K=32 fit with dead-feature revival on the same 64 items improved held-out fidelity to 0.847 and 0.665. We increased the corpus to 192 items and the dictionary to 384 atoms with K=32, and tested both 32-step pooling and native eight-step windows. The resulting dictionary settings are engineering choices informed by the initial diagnostics. Measurements are on the project’s sealed synthetic public-dev panel and are conditional on that training/held-out split. This integration exercise supplies no evidence of real-series forecasting skill or population-wide SAE interpretability. Those are measured separately in the research studies.

Before measuring the final native-token middle-layer gate, we designated large block 1 as the fallback. The first 32 principal components of the initial 51 training series explained 77.4% of the variance at observed block 11 and 93.0% at block 1. The final large middle dictionary explained 0.790 of held-out variance and failed the 0.85 fidelity criterion. At block 1 the same architecture and training recipe achieved 0.928, with held-out token-replacement ΔMASE +0.014 and 25.5% dead training atoms. Both criteria also passed (absolute ΔMASE ≤0.25 and dead fraction ≤0.30). The large config therefore targets early block 1. The small config’s middle block 3 passed all three criteria with fidelity 0.885, ΔMASE −0.0019 and zero dead training atoms. Each final dictionary uses 9,240 training tokens from 154 whole histories. The remaining 38 histories supply 2,280 held-out tokens. The standard preservation check samples eight whole histories from that held-out set. The separate +0.05-history-SD level request completed and replayed in all four held-out requests for each checkpoint. At the pre-existing relative tolerance of 10%, small met the requested output on 4/4 cases (median error 6.88%) and large on 2/4 (13.54%). Large and small used identical model-specific steering settings and data across the pooling comparisons. The steering command runs without an SAE dependency. We verified equality of the model specification, candidate-search settings, dataset and random seed when using results from separate stage invocations.

The profile-matched command nominated four candidates per checkpoint. Three small candidates and two large candidates fired on the selected histories and could be scored. The other random candidates had no eligible firing series. An independent physical check reimplemented rectified TopK encoding and decoding from the saved SAE tensors, then hooked only the 60 observed positions of the official public model. On four stored histories per checkpoint, the ordinary forecast, full per-token reconstruction forecast, and forecast with a single active feature removed all matched the CLI artifact to within **3.56e−7 history SD** for small and **8.02e−7** for large. Removing these particular naturally active features changed at least one forecast lead by 0.070 and 1.141 history SD respectively relative to the full-reconstruction baseline. The examples were selected for strong firing, and small/large use distinct layers and dictionaries. Their magnitudes have no direct cross-model feature equivalence. In this validation the large native-token run was interrupted by SIGTERM during L0, after extraction. We resumed the SAE from the completed normal activation store, used the independently completed L0 and steering stages on the exact same large checkpoint and 192 histories, and verified matching specifications, data, seed and steering options. For the fallback early dictionary we reused the native-token activation store as an independent copy. The standard commands above group the normal stages in one invocation. For the small checkpoint, substitute `configs/moment.yaml` and `runs/moment_small`. Use an environment with the dependencies in the [adapter instructions](README.md). All runs here used CPU float32.

In a separate packaging check, we built the Lens wheel from a temporary source copy, installed it into a fresh destination and imported it with the working checkout off the module path. The registered adapter imported its optional model package only when forecasting. Omitting the checkpoint selected the pinned large revision from an isolated offline Hub cache. Sixteen raw-temperature histories at H8 and H16 matched every archived native value, including the point replicas in the quantile array.
