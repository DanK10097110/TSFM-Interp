# Four-view crosscoder trained on observed patches at two depths

Freeze before measuring any four-view fit. The four views, in this order, are small middle, large middle, small final-layer norm, large final-layer norm. Use exactly the original first 768 M4 histories and the 60 observed eight-value patch slots per history (native indices 2 through 61). Final-layer supervision is an ordinary internal representation of a past patch. No future-slot hidden state, forecast, target-series future, native deletion labels or held-out dose selection enters this optimization.

Use 1,024 coordinates, per-row ReLU/TopK 32, 1,000 Adam updates at .001 with batch 256, the previous masked regime and the same two training seeds 22112401/22112402. Preserve the historical random row/position order and the same seed-specific schedule of exactly 500 source-only updates. On such updates only small-middle enters the shared code. All four raw centered views provide reconstruction losses on every update with equal coefficients after dividing each view by its own training RMS. Full updates encode all four. The control uses the same deterministic item derangement as the previous masked shuffled study, applied jointly to *both final views*. The two middle views always share the original history; small and large final remain paired to one another and the original physical patch position is fixed. Only the middle-to-final history identity is broken. Held-out evaluation uses actual, aligned final states for both methods.

Report source-only and four-view reconstruction R² at the last observed slot of the last 256 M4 histories, each relative to that view’s training mean. Keep source-only endpoint codes for all 1,024 M4 items, the fixed 35 × 11 NOAA panel and the 128+128 M4/traffic blind items. Retain true-unit decoders at both middle sites, indexing contracts, full model parameters and training normalization checks. Every evaluation code uses only small-middle at test. Compare to two-view masked reconstruction descriptively. Four rather than two views add trainable weights, decoder supervision and compute; equal latent width, batch count and token budget do not equalize these resources. No extra directional or native-intervention experiment belongs to the four training fits.
