# Confirm the H16-versus-H8 prefix effect on additional forecast dates

The four seasonal USCRN dates showed that the released MOMENT-large H16 prefix was less accurate for the same first eight temperatures than its direct H8 call. Freeze an additional date test before any forecasts on these dates. Use the first day of each month January through November 2026, scoring days 1–8 only. None of these 88 target dates appears in the original 64 temperature outcome dates. Historical inputs overlap substantially across dates. Primary cohort is every previously chosen station meeting the same completeness rules at all eleven new origins. Secondary cohort is previously unused contiguous-US stations meeting those rules, greedily retaining stations separated by 200 km within that cohort in fixed SHA256 order. Spatial independence from the primary cohort is not required. Report site counts and geography, including exclusions; no selection on forecast outcome magnitudes.

All models see 480 observed days, in degrees Celsius. Preserve the original training-only station climatology and one-sided fill convention; require eight finite target days, at most nine missing observed days and no eight-day consecutive input gap at each origin. Primary outcome is the paired station/date mean absolute error difference, large H16 prefix minus direct H8. Also test H32 (the previously selected six-domain policy), small H8/H16 and pre-2024 climatology plus decaying last anomaly. Report mean per-origin waveform discrepancy, number of positive date-mean contrasts, 11 leave-one-date-out values and independent whole-date, whole-station, and crossed percentile resampling. Four fixed original dates motivated this test. Eleven dates from one year limit temporal inference; a smaller or opposite accuracy effect would bound the initial result while leaving graph-level prefix inconsistency testable.
