# Post-release US weather observations

Registered on November 22, 2026 before using any station observations or forecasts. Use NOAA US Climate Reference Network daily01 snapshot CRND0103-202611160850. The study variable is T_DAILY_AVG, daily average air temperature in degrees Celsius (USCRN field 9). The next eight and sixteen calendar days are forecast directly, from 480 daily observations ending the preceding date. Four future intervals start January 10, April 10, July 10 and October 10 of 2026. This places every scored observation later than all five dated forecasting checkpoint releases. Historic weather data were public and may have entered training.

The candidate roster is every 2026 USCRN station with a filename in a contiguous US state. Order filenames by SHA-256 of 'moment-noaa-2026|' plus station name. Scan in order, requiring station coordinates at least 200 km from any previously accepted station (haversine); cap at 64. Eligibility requires all four test intervals completely observed, at most nine missing values and no run exceeding seven days in each 480-day context, and at least eight years with 300 valid temperature days during 2008–2023. Check these as availability filters before applying geographic separation, then freeze the resulting exact stations and date ranges in a manifest before model calls. The source historic reference climatology is station-specific within a circular ±7-calendar-day window across 2008–2023; use fixed leap-year coordinates. Forecast controls are persistence, 365-day seasonal persistence, full-history drift, historical climatology and climatology plus a one-step autoregressive decaying anomaly. Fit the latter persistence parameter from consecutive pre-2024 daily climatology residuals, constrain it to [0,1]. Fill any missing historical input in chronological order with this same known seasonal climate plus the decayed last observed anomaly, never using later values. No label, imputation or model adaptation uses post-origin observations.

Five checkpoints are MOMENT small, base and large, Chronos-Bolt and TimesFM. Each receives the same 480-value context in raw degrees C, with no dates or places, and outputs its own direct H8 and H16 median/point prediction. Station/date-dependent climatology controls have extra calendar information. Primary outcome is degrees-C MAE averaged within station across four origins and then across stations; also report MASE1, late leads, regional results and equal-weighted forecast-origin MAE. Paired bootstrap uses stations as blocks (4,000 draws). A supplementary four-region Census block bootstrap exposes spatial dependence; four dates and four regions do not justify broad weather-generalization claims. Publish source and hashes, all IDs and raw input/target arrays, forecast arrays, and metrics. No fitted forecast head or selection by forecast outcome.

Before any NOAA model scoring, add leave-one-date and leave-one-region-out raw MAE contrasts to the recorded geographic/date breakdown. These describe dependence on the four particular target intervals and region composition.
