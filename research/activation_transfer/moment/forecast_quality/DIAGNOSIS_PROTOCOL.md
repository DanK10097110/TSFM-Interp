# Reconstruction controls after the anomalous small forecast

The small H8 test failed persistence in five of six domains, and initial inspection of its published head found the initializer's orthogonal row geometry. This motivates a diagnostic, separate from the six-domain primary endpoint. Freeze the first 12 diagnostic items per domain and the later of their two histories. Hide the same 8 real observations (indices 416:424 of each 480-value context) in each condition. Score against those observed points at all three MOMENT sizes.

Compare the native causal short forecast from the prefix, an independently assembled terminal masked slot with the same prefix, the same causal evidence at an interior position, the interior slot with all 56 genuine later observations, with a different diagnostic item's standardized right flank (fixed within-domain cyclic shift), and with the actual eight points visible as a privileged reconstruction control. Hold the prefix's native RevIN statistics fixed in the manual paths so positional and right-evidence effects cannot result from different scales. Include actual official masked interior reconstruction, which uses both flanks for normalization. Compare causal last and prefix mean, and an explicitly noncausal two-boundary linear interpolation. Report per-domain level correlation, history-scaled MAE and slope, and head geometry from original released safetensors. No learned decoder. This can diagnose public checkpoint behavior. Head geometry alone cannot establish how or when a release was produced.

None reproduced the released row direction; absolute flattened cosine stayed below 0.022. The production architecture consumes random draws in other modules before the head and historical RNG state is unknown. We therefore report the initializer's *Gram geometry*, without claiming an exact recovered seed or a known release history.

Before scoring the reconstruction diagnostics, add 4,000 independently within-domain resamples of the 12 selected series to the paired MAE/SD contrasts. Keep each series intact and average six domain draws equally. Compare native causal to persistence, interior prefix-only to terminal, genuine to wrong or absent right evidence, visible reconstruction to prefix mean, and native masked interior to two-sided interpolation.

It predicts the mean of the 416 visible preceding and 56 visible following points, with no network. Decompose the native forecast level into the shift in this observed mean and the residual around that mean.
