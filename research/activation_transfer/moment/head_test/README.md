# What the smaller public heads leave in the frozen encoder

Replacing only the pointwise decoder of **MOMENT-small**, using labels from interior holes in 1,024 separate historical daily series, reduced daily H16 forecast error from **19.23 to 4.22 MASE1**. The paired error ratio was **.220 [.192, .255]**; the fitted head improved over the published head on all 96 held-out daily evaluation series. Persistence achieved **2.57** on the same test. The readout recovered useful information while leaving sizeable forecasting errors. We masked eight interior patches in every 480-step training history, omitted those labels from the model's conditioning **and from its normalization**, and fitted a shared eight-output linear decoder to the final hidden state at each masked patch. The 128 validation series were disjoint and selected ridge strength using only interior reconstruction. The NOAA panel contains four 2026 origins at each of 46 stations and contributed no labels to fitting. A station-specific calendar baseline with recent anomaly achieved 3.65°C. The two smaller checkpoints' weak published reconstruction and the possibility of recovering patch information with a base ridge head were previously reported. [Independent October 2026 report](https://github.com/moment-timeseries-foundation-model/moment/issues/87) · [Raw weather results](results/post_release.json)

**Where was the information?** On daily M4, a fit restricted to the null space of small's eight published decoder rows reached 4.16 MASE1. Fitting only a recalibration of those eight published outputs yielded 5.73. The orthogonal complement has 504 dimensions, so this experiment demonstrates availability outside the eight rows, without attributing that information to any specific unit. Identically fitted small encoders with two random initializations reached 15.63 and 9.76. Base's corresponding true-future scores were 4.00 for the complement and 3.95 for the recalibrated published outputs. The daily result specific to small cannot be generalized to every checkpoint. [Independent linear algebra and real model replacement](independent/README.md)

On the disjoint interior validation task, the fitted small/base/large heads reached R² .950/.951/.935. The published small/base/large heads scored −.304/−.146/.861. Large can lose forecasting accuracy after the same change, as the hourly traffic result illustrates. A second locally fitted head used the true terminal 16 values from the historical training series as training labels, preserving the same masked inference architecture. A further experiment used the official forecasting-task architecture that reads **all** encoder tokens instead. We identify these three objectives separately when reporting forecasts and interventions.
