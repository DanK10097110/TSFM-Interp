\section{Experimental Setup}
\label{sec:setup}

\paragraph{Models.} We analyze seven TSFMs (Table~\ref{tab:models}): TimesFM~2.5 \citep{das2024timesfm}, Chronos-2
\citep{ansari2025chronos2}, Sundial \citep{liu2025sundial}, Chronos-Bolt and Chronos-T5-Base \citep{ansari2024chronos},
Timer \citep{liu2024timer} and Time-MoE \citep{shi2025timemoe}. Timer and Time-MoE are loaded through the zero-code
adapter. Together the panel covers decoder-only, encoder-only and encoder--decoder stacks, token widths from 1 to 96
steps, a mixture-of-experts model and deterministic and sampled forecast heads.

\paragraph{Corpora.} All claims are tested on two independently generated corpora, each with its own dev split, sealed
private split and registry.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \textbf{v2} (primary). The dev split has 965 series in five generator families; 410 of them are real-derived,
    built from Monash weather, ETT \citep{zhou2021informer} and the Chronos electricity and traffic collections. The
    private split is a sealed epoch of 971 series that shares no sample with any earlier split. Dev and private splits
    are exchangeable: an energy-distance test gives $p=0.951$, 20 of 22 catch22 features are equivalent by TOST and the
    other two are inconclusive, and 6 of 937{,}015 cross-split pairs are near-duplicates.
  \item \textbf{v3} (replication, three times larger). The dev split has 2{,}876 series, 2{,}091 synthetic and 785
    real-derived from 15 real sources, adding ERCOT load, M4 hourly, solar, taxi, KDD Cup air quality and pedestrian
    counts to the v2 sources. The private split is 1{,}024 series drawn from a sealed epoch of 2{,}868. An
    \emph{external} set of 800 raw windows from 12 GIFT-Eval datasets \citep{aksu2024gifteval}, cut without any
    generator and sharing no sample with either split, re-tests the registered transfer claims and is reported separately
    from the confirmation verdict. Exchangeability, computed after the confirmation look and without any model, gives
    energy-distance $p=0.995$ and 22 of 22 features equivalent.
\end{itemize}
Contexts have 480 steps, a multiple of Timer's 96-step token, the horizon is 64 steps and the alignment window 32 steps.

\paragraph{Protocol.} On v2, SAEs are trained on 56 target layers (TimesFM 10, Chronos-2 9, Sundial 9, Time-MoE 9,
Chronos-T5-Base 9, Timer 6 and Chronos-Bolt 4), each with three replicate seeds, and causal features are scored against
the profile-matched null. On v3, SAEs are trained on 50 targets and every causal claim is scored and registered against
the stricter covariance-matched null; every setting of the v3 run was fixed before its data existed. Bootstrap
intervals use $n_{\text{boot}}=5000$. The v2 registry holds 128 hypotheses, 82 of them replicable on the private split;
the v3 registry holds 111, of which 63 are replicable. Each private split was opened once.

\paragraph{Sources of numbers.} Unless stated otherwise, numbers are from the v2 dev run or its confirmation. Numbers
from v3 are labeled as such. The v2 run used 21.1 hours of stage time on one RTX A5000 (24~GB); its artifacts
are in \repodir{examples/panel7_v2/run}, and Appendix~\ref{app:repro} gives the full provenance.

\paragraph{How the models behave on this corpus.} Interpretability results are only meaningful if the models do the
task they were trained for. Table~\ref{tab:character} summarizes their behavior on the v2 dev split. Chronos-2 is the most
accurate. Five models beat the seasonal-naive forecast on 76.5--84.5\% of series, Timer on 69.5\% and Time-MoE on only
37.8\%; Time-MoE is less accurate than the naive forecast overall, and its results should be read with this in mind.
The nominal 80\% intervals of Sundial and Chronos-T5-Base cover only 36\% and 44\% of outcomes, and four models propagate
an injected missing value silently. On the private split, Chronos-2 is more accurate than TimesFM on the registered
\texttt{mixture} family ($\Delta$MASE 0.209 [0.119, 0.313], $p_{\text{Holm}}=0.0002$, $n=290$) and, with a smaller effect,
overall ($-0.070$ [$-0.129$, $-0.008$], $p=0.025$, $n=971$). On v3 the \texttt{mixture} result replicates (0.303 [0.176,
0.445], $p_{\text{Holm}}=0.0002$).
