\section{Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Time-series foundation models.} TimesFM \citep{das2024timesfm} is a decoder-only model over 32-step patches.
Chronos \citep{ansari2024chronos} quantizes values and trains a T5 encoder--decoder \citep{raffel2020t5} with a
sampled decoder; Chronos-Bolt replaces the decoder with a direct patch head, and Chronos-2 \citep{ansari2025chronos2} is
an encoder with cross-series group attention. Sundial \citep{liu2025sundial} samples from a flow-matching head
\citep{lipman2023flow}, Timer \citep{liu2024timer} uses 96-step tokens, Time-MoE \citep{shi2025timemoe} is a sparse
mixture of experts over per-step tokens, and Lag-Llama \citep{rasul2023lagllama} reads lagged values. Most of these
models normalize each series internally \citep{kim2022revin}, a detail an adapter must reproduce when it calls a model
below its public API. Synthetic pretraining and evaluation data are common in this literature
\citep{kang2020gratis,dooley2023forecastpfn}; we use synthetic data instead to obtain ground truth for interpretation.
\citet{wan2026collapse} document flat, ``collapsed'' forecasts on weakly predictable targets, which we also observe
(\fid{PM-07}).

\paragraph{Interpreting time-series models.} Saliency methods for time-series classifiers are unreliable across
architectures \citep{ismail2020benchmarking}, and \citet{kalnare2025mechts} apply patching and SAEs to time-series
classifiers. For TSFMs, \citet{wilinski2025exploring} study representational redundancy and concept steering, and four
recent studies train SAEs on a single TSFM \citep{mishra2026dissecting,oublal2026timesae,divo2026zeitgeist,
yildirim2026superposition}. Each examines one model or one method. None compares architectures on a common time axis,
validates its probes against generative ground truth, separates input agreement from effect agreement, or tests its
findings on held-out data.

\paragraph{Validity of interpretability methods.} Probes need control tasks to separate what a representation encodes
from what the probe learns \citep{hewitt2019control,belinkov2022probing}. Activation patching is sensitive to the choice
of metric and corruption \citep{zhang2024patching,heimersheim2024patching}, subspace interventions can create illusory
mechanisms \citep{makelov2024illusion}, and the apparent meaning of a direction can depend on the data used to inspect
it \citep{bolukbasi2021illusion}. Sanity checks show that
attributions can be insensitive to the model they explain \citep{adebayo2018sanity}. Attention weights are not
explanations \citep{jain2019attention}. Sparse autoencoders are motivated by superposition
\citep{elhage2022superposition,cunningham2024sae,templeton2024scaling}, but their features vary across seeds
\citep{paulo2025seeds}, and crosscoders have been proposed to share one dictionary across models
\citep{lindsey2024crosscoders}. Causal abstraction \citep{geiger2021causal} formalizes when an intervention supports a
mechanistic claim. Our known-answer tests address the same concern empirically: each instrument must recover a planted
answer before it is applied to a real model.

\paragraph{Comparing representations.} Representational similarity
\citep{raghu2017svcca,morcos2018pwcca,kornblith2019cka,kriegeskorte2008rsa} and model stitching
\citep{lenc2015equivariance,bansal2021stitching} underlie claims that different networks converge on similar
representations \citep{li2016convergent,huh2024platonic}. We use these measures only against shuffled-series nulls and
show that, for TSFMs, they disagree with one another and with causal measures.

\paragraph{Tooling and research practice.} Libraries such as TransformerLens, nnsight
\citep{fiottokaufman2025nnsight}, pyvene, SAELens \citep{bloom2024saelens} and Captum \citep{kokhlikyan2020captum}
provide primitives for one model, but not comparative designs, nulls, multiplicity control or confirmation
(Table~\ref{tab:tools}). From empirical science we adopt pre-registration \citep{nosek2018preregistration}, protection
against adaptive reuse of holdouts \citep{dwork2015reusable} and against analytic flexibility
\citep{gelman2014garden,simonsohn2020specification}, leakage auditing \citep{kapoor2023leakage} and series-level
resampling \citep{efron1993bootstrap,cameron2008cluster}.
