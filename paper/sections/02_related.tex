\section{Background and Related Work}
\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Time-series foundation models.} TSFMs are transformers pretrained on large corpora of real
and synthetic series and applied zero-shot. They differ in almost every design decision that matters
for interpretability. TimesFM \citep{das2024timesfm} is a decoder-only stack over 32-step input patches
that emits 128-step output patches. Chronos \citep{ansari2024chronos} quantizes scaled values into a
vocabulary and trains a T5 encoder--decoder \citep{raffel2020t5} with one token per step and a sampled
decoder; Chronos-Bolt replaces the decoder with a direct patch head, and Chronos-2
\citep{ansari2025chronos2} is an encoder over 16-step patches that adds cross-series ``group''
attention. Sundial \citep{liu2025sundial} is decoder-only with a flow-matching head
\citep{lipman2023flow} that samples forecasts. Timer \citep{liu2024timer} uses 96-step tokens,
Lag-Llama \citep{rasul2023lagllama} reads a set of lagged values rather than a contiguous window, and
Moirai \citep{woo2024moirai}, MOMENT \citep{goswami2024moment}, Time-MoE \citep{shi2025timemoe} and
TTM \citep{ekambaram2024ttm} add further variation in patching, masking and mixture-of-experts
routing. Most of these models normalize each series internally, in the manner of reversible instance
normalization \citep{kim2022revin}. This detail turns out to matter: an adapter that calls a model
below its public API can silently skip that normalization (Section~\ref{sec:controls}). Synthetic
pretraining data are increasingly common \citep{kang2020gratis,dooley2023forecastpfn}, and
\citet{wan2026collapse} recently documented ``forecast collapse'' to flat predictions on weakly
predictable targets, a behavior we also measure (\fid{PM-07}, \fid{BM-06}).

\paragraph{Interpretability of time-series models and TSFMs.} Saliency methods for time-series
classifiers were benchmarked by \citet{ismail2020benchmarking}, who found them unreliable across
architectures. \citet{kalnare2025mechts} applied activation patching, attention saliency and SAEs to
time-series \emph{classification} transformers. For TSFMs, \citet{wilinski2025exploring} analyzed
block-wise representational redundancy and steered forecasts with concept directions derived from
synthetic data. Four recent studies train sparse autoencoders on a single TSFM:
\citet{mishra2026dissecting} ablates TopK-SAE features of Chronos-T5-Large and reports a depth
hierarchy of causal importance; \citet{oublal2026timesae} adapt SAEs to explain black-box time-series
models; \citet{divo2026zeitgeist} look for monosemantic features in pretrained TSFMs; and
\citet{yildirim2026superposition} argue that PatchTST \citep{nie2023patchtst} does not rely on
superposition. These studies each examine one model or one method. None of them compares
architectures on a common time axis, validates probes against generative ground truth, separates
input agreement from effect agreement across models, or tests findings on held-out data. \tool{} is
complementary: it is a system for doing all of these at once, and its case study reaches some
conclusions that a single-model analysis cannot (for example, that multi-model concepts are mostly
convergent rather than shared; Section~\ref{sec:rq2}).

\paragraph{Mechanistic interpretability methods and tooling.} Linear probes
\citep{alain2017probes} need control tasks to separate what a representation encodes from what the
probe learns \citep{hewitt2019control,belinkov2022probing}. Activation patching and causal mediation
\citep{vig2020causal,meng2022rome,geiger2021causal,wang2023ioi} are sensitive to metric and
corruption choices \citep{zhang2024patching,heimersheim2024patching} and can create illusory subspaces
\citep{makelov2024illusion}. The logit lens and tuned lens
\citep{nostalgebraist2020logitlens,belrose2023tuned} read predictions out of intermediate layers.
Sparse dictionaries \citep{makhzani2013ksparse,bricken2023monosemanticity,cunningham2024sae,
templeton2024scaling,gao2025topk} address superposition \citep{elhage2022superposition}, and
crosscoders \citep{lindsey2024crosscoders} learn features jointly across layers or models; SAE features
are known to vary across training seeds \citep{paulo2025seeds}. Libraries such as TransformerLens
\citep{nanda2022transformerlens}, nnsight \citep{fiottokaufman2025nnsight}, pyvene \citep{wu2024pyvene},
SAELens \citep{bloom2024saelens} and Captum \citep{kokhlikyan2020captum} make these methods easy to
apply to one model. They are microscopes: they provide primitives, not comparative designs, nulls,
multiplicity control or confirmation. \tool{} uses the same primitives (hooks, patching, TopK SAEs)
but wraps them in the experimental design needed for comparative claims.

\paragraph{Representation similarity and universality.} Whether different networks learn the same
representations has been studied through convergent learning \citep{li2016convergent}, SVCCA and
PWCCA \citep{raghu2017svcca,morcos2018pwcca}, CKA \citep{kornblith2019cka}, RSA
\citep{kriegeskorte2008rsa} and model stitching \citep{lenc2015equivariance,bansal2021stitching}, and
has culminated in claims of universality \citep{olah2020zoom} and convergence
\citep{huh2024platonic}. Our results add a caution specific to TSFMs: similarity metrics rank model
pairs inconsistently, a randomly initialized pair can look more similar than a genuinely related
trained pair, and unit-level correspondence measures fail rotation controls because TSFM
representations are extremely low-rank (Section~\ref{sec:rq2}).

\paragraph{Validity of empirical claims.} Sanity checks for saliency \citep{adebayo2018sanity},
interpretability illusions \citep{bolukbasi2021illusion,makelov2024illusion}, the gap between attention
weight and importance \citep{jain2019attention} and ablation studies of individual units
\citep{zhou2018revisiting} all show that interpretability outputs can look meaningful without being
so. More broadly, adaptive reuse of data inflates error rates
\citep{gelman2014garden,dwork2015reusable}, leakage undermines ML-based science
\citep{kapoor2023leakage}, results can fail to transfer to fresh test sets \citep{recht2019imagenet},
and pre-registration is the standard remedy \citep{nosek2018preregistration}. \tool{} imports these
remedies directly: registered hypotheses, a sealed split used once, specification curves
\citep{simonsohn2020specification} for analysis-knob robustness, and resampling at the level of the
independent unit \citep{efron1993bootstrap,cameron2008cluster}.
