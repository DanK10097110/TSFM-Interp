\section{Additional Validity Analyses}
\label{app:faq}

\paragraph{Benchmark quality.} Every v2 sample passed the banded-DTW leakage gate (threshold 0.35) against Monash: 68 of
2{,}000 candidates were rejected, and the closest accepted sample sits at distance 0.352. Between dev and private splits,
tier and family composition are indistinguishable ($p=0.94$ and $p=0.9999$) and 6 of 937{,}015 cross-split pairs are
near-duplicates (v3: 9 of 8{,}248{,}368). The dev split has effective catch22 dimension 5.37 of 22 (private 5.57) and a
near-collision fraction of 0.052.

\paragraph{Statistical power.} Each claim's power is stated. The 20 transfer claims all reach the Holm floor; the
accuracy claim's minimum detectable $\Delta$MASE is 0.133 against an observed 0.209; the held-out concatenated CKA of
TimesFM and Chronos-2 is 0.707 [0.679, 0.734] against a null 95th percentile of 0.010. Single-feature causal claims are
power-limited: each feature is tested on its 8 strongest-firing series, and 19 of the 21 that did not confirm never
reached the dev $p$ floor. The three times larger v3 corpus reproduces the held-out pattern (41 of 63 claims).

\paragraph{Robustness of the dictionaries.} Each target has three replicate dictionaries, and 9 of 11 atlas concepts are
stable across them. Held-out fraction of variance explained has medians of 0.647 (TimesFM) to 0.878 (Chronos-T5-Base). The
ablation keeps the dictionary's error in both arms, so a measured effect is a real intervention whatever the fidelity, and
fidelity does not predict causal clarity across v3 targets ($\rho=-0.326$, $n=49$; \fid{MN-34}). For the comparison of
interventions in Section~\ref{sec:rq1} the v3 dictionaries were retrained from the recorded configuration; training
reproduced all 50 dictionaries exactly (identical loss histories) and the same 91 features clear.

\paragraph{Sampled models.} Every compared pair of calls to Sundial or Chronos-T5 uses the same seed: unseeded,
back-to-back Sundial calls differ by up to 0.50 MASE (\fid{PM-09}). Repeat-run noise floors are reported per model (Sundial
0.14, Chronos-T5-Base 0.24 mean $|\Delta$MASE$|$, the deterministic models 0).

\paragraph{Integrity of the confirmation.} The v2 registry (128 hypotheses, sha256 recorded) was frozen before the private
epoch was generated; the epoch shares no sample hash with any of seven earlier splits, and \texttt{confirm} ran once.
Registered claims are cuts from larger dev pools (transfer 20 of 1{,}124 candidates, causal 32 of 113, agreement 11 of 25,
atlas 4 of the 9 stable concepts). The v3 registry (111 hypotheses, sha256 \texttt{67b91269\ldots}) was frozen in the same
way, and the window-geometry claims were registered (sha256 \texttt{cda4f47a\ldots}) before their epoch was extracted.

\section{Statistical Details}
\label{app:stats}

\paragraph{Paired series bootstrap.} With per-series differences $d_s=m_{a,s}-m_{b,s}$ and $B=n_{\text{boot}}$
resampled means $\bar d^*_b$, the interval is the percentile interval and
$p=\min\!\big(1,\max(\tfrac1B,\,2\min\{\tfrac1B\#(\bar d^*_b\le 0),\tfrac1B\#(\bar d^*_b\ge 0)\})\big)$
(\texttt{analysis/stats.py}; floor per \citealp{phipson2010permutation}). Window-level statistics (CKA,
fingerprints) resample series with all their windows \citep{cameron2008cluster}.

\paragraph{Multiplicity.} Holm: $\tilde p_{(i)}=\max_{j\le i}\min(1,(m-j+1)p_{(j)})$ \citep{holm1979}; with
$p\ge 1/B$ the smallest attainable value is $m/B$, which the ledger reports per family, flagging $m/B>\alpha$.
BH: $\tilde q_{(i)}=\min_{j\ge i}\min(1,\tfrac{m}{j}p_{(j)})$ \citep{benjamini1995fdr}.

\paragraph{Profile-matched ablation null.} For feature $i$ with rows $R_i$ and $J=16$ unit directions $u_j$, the null
removes at every token $t$ the amount $|z_i(t)|\,\|w_i\|\,u_j$; $u_j$ is $W_d^{\top}r_j$ normalized, with $r_j$ a random unit code. The covariance-matched null instead uses
$u_j\propto\sum_t g_{jt}(x_t-\bar x)$ with $g_{jt}\sim\mathcal{N}(0,1)$ over the chunk's clean token activations $x_t$,
a draw from a Gaussian with their sample covariance. Channel $c$ clears if
$\frac{1}{|R_i|}\sum_{r\in R_i}|\Delta^{(c)}_{i,r}| > q_{0.95}\{|\Delta^{(c)}_{u_j,r}|: j\le J, r\in R_i\}$. A null
with no spread is reported as degenerate and not scored.

\paragraph{Transfer.} The statistic is $\max_f \mathrm{AUC}(a_f;S)$ over destination features; its null
recomputes the same maximum on stratum-matched random $k$-sets, so the feature search is inside the null. The
reverse leg scores the chosen feature's own top-$k$ by the source activation. $p=(1+\#\{\text{null}\ge
\text{obs}\})/(1+n)$; a registered claim uses $\max(p,p_{\text{rev}})$ (intersection--union), Holm across claims.

\paragraph{Shared-input agreement.} On $U$, statistic (i) is the Spearman concordance of the two sides' signed
level effects across series, and (ii) the cosine of their level-removed shape vectors, each channel in its own
null units. Each side's floor ablates random feature sets matched to its activation on $U$ and re-scores against
the other side's measured effect; agreement exceeds both floors' $q_{0.95}$, ``acts differently'' falls below
both $q_{0.05}$.

\paragraph{Minimum detectable effect.} Before the private result is read, recentred per-series differences are
shifted by a candidate effect and re-tested with the same bootstrap; bisection finds the smallest shift detected
with power 0.8 (\texttt{analysis/power.py}).

\section{Reproducibility}
\label{app:repro}

\repodir{examples/panel7_v2} holds the configuration, the report and every stage's JSON/parquet artifacts plus the
confirmation (activations and SAE weights omitted for size); \texttt{paper/figures/make\_figures.py -{}-run DIR}
regenerates all data figures. From \texttt{tsfm\_lens/}, \texttt{python run.py -{}-config configs/panel7\_v2.yaml}
reproduces the run (\texttt{-{}-stages report} re-renders it, \texttt{-{}-verify-provenance} diffs environments). The v2
private split (epoch 3, 971 series) was opened once on 2026-10-02 and the v3 split (1{,}024 series) on 2026-10-10. One
event is disclosed: the first v2 confirm attempt crashed while assembling the claim ledger, after the private batteries
finished but before any statistic was written; no private number was read, the registry and every frozen specification
were unchanged, and the artifact records \texttt{repeated\_look: false}. Environment (\texttt{run\_manifest.json}):
Python 3.12.13, numpy 2.1.0, PyTorch 2.12.0 (CUDA 12.9), transformers 4.57.6, timesfm 2.0.2, chronos-forecasting 2.3.1,
zarr 2.18.7, scikit-learn 1.7.2; pins and reasons are in \repo{DEPENDENCIES.md}. Tests: 2{,}647 (\texttt{tsfm\_lens}) and
161 (\texttt{tsfm\_benchmark}), run in continuous integration. Stage wall-clock on one RTX A5000: 75{,}900 s, of which
\texttt{sae} 14{,}614 s and \texttt{concepts} 55{,}608 s.
