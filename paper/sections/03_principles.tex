\section{Design Principles}
\label{sec:principles}

Each principle corresponds to a failure that actually happened (Section~\ref{sec:controls}) and is enforced
in code as a gate, refusal or test.
\begin{description}[leftmargin=1.2em,font=\normalfont\bfseries,itemsep=1pt]
\item[P1. Ground truth by construction.] Every probe is first validated on a case whose answer is known: the
  benchmark records every generative component of every synthetic series, and every perturbation is applied
  to series whose components are known.
\item[P2. Alignment is measured, not declared.] Token spans are measured from impulse responses; cross-model
  analysis happens only on a common window axis; models that are not time-localized receive behavioral
  analysis only.
\item[P3. Causal claims need a null, a reach control and a same-seed baseline.] Every intervention is compared
  with a random intervention of matched size on the same series; self-patching must change the forecast by
  exactly $0.0$ and cross-patching by a nonzero amount; sampled models are seeded identically in compared calls.
\item[P4. The series is the unit of inference.] All intervals and $p$-values resample series; $p$-values are
  floored at $1/n_{\text{boot}}$, and each correction family's attainable minimum is checked.
\item[P5. Explore, then confirm once.] Dev results are exploratory; registered claims are tested once on a
  sealed split, and a second look is recorded.
\item[P6. Every claim is typed; failures are loud.] Each finding carries an evidence class
  (\ev{behavioral}, \ev{descriptive}, \ev{geometric}, \ev{linear-translatable}, \ev{causal within-model},
  \ev{illustrative}, \ev{held-out confirmed}) and a generated caveat; unsupported analyses are skipped with a
  rendered reason.
\item[P7. Written for newcomers.] The deliverable is one HTML file, designed with the intention that a reader new
  to interpretability can follow it, with every figure explained and every verdict derived from artifacts rather than written by hand.
\end{description}
New behavior is opt-in so sealed corpora regenerate bit-exactly, and stage configurations are fingerprinted
so stale cached artifacts are refused.
